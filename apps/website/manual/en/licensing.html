MIXRELAY / MANUAL

Free edition and licensing

The free edition has no time limit. A paid license expands the same installed product.

Prerelease draft. Public downloads and purchases are not available yet. These instructions describe the current source build; installed behavior still needs verification in the environments noted below.

On this page

  • Free edition limits

01

Free edition limits

Graphs
One graph, with saving, loading and restoration.
Inputs
Two physical-input or individual app-capture nodes in total. Virtual-device sources do not count toward this input limit.
Outputs
Two distinct output devices, including virtual output devices. Repeated references to the same device count once. Recorders do not count as output devices.
Virtual devices and mappings
One virtual device. Four channel connections from editing graphs into virtual devices in total; one stereo pair counts as two. Connections between effects or into physical outputs and recorders do not count toward this mapping limit.
Effects
Unlimited built-in effects with parameter and preset editing. Two external plug-in instances, including instances that are off.
Recording
One recorder, up to two channels and two recorded hours per take.

The free Driver device supports up to 64 channels per direction with its supported sample rates and PCM settings. Basic loopback and device use by external DAWs are separate from the editor’s four-mapping limit. Device, graph and mapping saving remain available within the free limits. Free use adds no noise, quality reduction or extra latency.

02

Paid access and buying

A paid license removes the free graph, input, output, mapping, plug-in and recorder quotas, while technical limits remain. It enables virtual-device creation and renaming.

The confirmed price is US$69 list price and US$39 ongoing sale price, excluding tax. Lemon Squeezy handles payment and key issuance. Applicable tax and the total must be shown before payment. The store and public purchase link are not available yet. When purchasing is enabled, choose Buy a license in License, complete checkout and use the issued key to activate. A build may show Purchases and activation are not available in this build.

The license covers one person on an unlimited number of their own computers. Do not share the key with others. Updates within the purchased major version are included; buying a later major version is optional. Personal and business routing, recording and broadcast use are allowed. OEM, bundling, resale, white-label use and integration into another product require a separate written agreement. Free use does not grant modification or redistribution rights.

03

Activate and check online

  1. Choose the version and license status at the lower left to open License.
  2. In a configured build, enter your License key and a Device name that identifies this computer.
  3. Choose Activate. Use Check license online for a manual online check.

Successful online verification permits 30 days of offline paid access from that success. Restarting or failed checks do not renew it. The dialog shows the remaining offline days and valid-until date. Background checks at runtime start or first app connection share a 24-hour attempt limit; there is no separate repeating license-check timer during a running session.

Connection failures do not erase valid authentication or stop ongoing audio and recordings. If access expires or is revoked, new paid operations require valid access. Existing work is preserved; excess configurations are not automatically deleted. Free features remain available.

04

Deactivate a computer

While online, open License and choose Deactivate this device on the computer you want to release. This removes that computer’s activation, not the key or other computers’ activations. Activate with the same key on your own other computer. Deactivating the old computer is not required to make room for another activation because your own computers are unlimited.

Reinstalling and remote deactivation are separate. Deactivate before uninstalling if you want to release the activation. If the old computer is unavailable or activation cannot be confirmed, contact support. Do not repeat activation when the app says support must resolve an uncertain result. Check the device clock if Device clock needs attention appears.

05

Refunds

Request a full refund within 30 calendar days of purchase, without giving a reason. Contact support@rawsector.com with the purchase reference. Statutory consumer rights remain. A full refund also deactivates the paid key. Free features remain available after a refund or device deactivation. An offline computer cannot receive a remote license update immediately.
