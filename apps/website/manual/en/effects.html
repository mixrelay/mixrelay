MIXRELAY / MANUAL

Built-in effects and presets

Built-in processing is available without an instance limit in the free edition.

Prerelease draft. Public downloads and purchases are not available yet. These instructions describe the current source build; installed behavior still needs verification in the environments noted below.

On this page

  • Add and adjust effects
  • Presets and effect chains

01

Add and adjust effects

Choose Add node → Processor, select an effect, then connect its input and output channels. Open Options for parameters. Available effects are Compressor, Limiter, Equalizer, High-pass, Low-pass, Gate, Expander, Channel utility, Mono pan, Stereo balance, Delay, Ducking, Reverb and Chorus.

  • Compressor and Limiter control dynamics and peaks. Their gain reduction meter uses engine measurements; a dash means no measurement is available.
  • Equalizer has three peak bands. Drag a point to change frequency and gain, or use the Frequency, Gain and Q fields in Options. High-pass and Low-pass remove low or high frequencies.
  • Gate and Expander reduce signals below their threshold. Channel utility offers mono sum, channel duplication, left/right swap and polarity inversion. Mono sum averages L and R.
  • Mono pan places a mono signal in stereo; Stereo balance adjusts left/right balance. Ducking uses a separate mono Control input to reduce the main signal. Connect the control source separately; it is not mixed into the output.
  • Delay adds timed echoes. Reverb and Chorus add space or modulation. Their channel layouts differ; connect the ports actually offered.

Bypass passes dry audio. Turning a node off blocks its signal. Built-in noise suppression and LUFS measurement are not available.

02

Presets and effect chains

Use Preset in an effect’s Options. Reverb includes Small room, Studio, Hall, Karaoke, Tunnel and Fishbowl. Delay includes Short echo, Slapback, Vocal echo, Karaoke, Long echo and Ping-pong. Editing a preset’s values changes it to Custom.

For several connected effects, choose Add node → Processor → Effect chains, then Karaoke, Tunnel, Fishbowl, Radio or Double vocal. A chain adds its own nodes and connections without rewiring existing cables. Connect your source and output to the chain. One Undo removes the insertion. Saved workspaces retain actual parameters and connections. No external impulse-response or sample installation is required.
