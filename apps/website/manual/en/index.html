MIXRELAY / MANUAL

User manual

Set up MixRelay, connect audio, record and manage your installation.

Prerelease draft. Public downloads and purchases are not available yet. These instructions describe the current source build; installed behaviour still needs verification in the environments noted below.

01

Manual chapters

  1. System requirements and installation

    Install the complete MixRelay product for your operating system. No separate Driver installation is needed.

  2. First launch and permissions

    Open MixRelay, wait for its audio engine, then select the sources you want to use.

  3. Routing workspace

    Build a graph from source, processor and output nodes. Cables carry audio between individual channels.

  4. Devices and virtual endpoints

    Use MixRelay IO to exchange audio with other apps. Its device format is separate from the recording format.

  5. App audio capture

    Capture a running application and route its audio through your graph.

  6. Recording

    Record any connected graph signal to an RF64 WAV file.

  7. Built-in effects and presets

    Built-in processing is available without an instance limit in the free edition.

  8. External plug-ins

    Install effects with their vendor’s installer, then discover them in MixRelay.

  9. Stream Deck plug-in

    Control graph nodes from Stream Deck on macOS.

  10. MIDI controllers

    Control nodes from a MIDI controller on macOS or Linux.

  11. Free edition and licensing

    The free edition has no time limit. A paid licence expands the same installed product.

  12. Updates

    Update MixRelay as one complete product. Updates are downloaded and installed manually.

  13. Uninstalling

    Remove the complete product with its uninstaller or Linux package manager.

  14. Troubleshooting and diagnostics

    Check the current message and signal path before changing or removing saved work.

  15. Support contact

    Contact Echo Vision Lab about MixRelay, licensing, refunds or product integration.
