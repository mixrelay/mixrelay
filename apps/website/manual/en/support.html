Contact support

Email support@mixrelay.com. The first-response target is within two Korean business days. This is a response target, not a guaranteed resolution time. Public sales and support-mail operations are still being prepared.

For a problem report, include your MixRelay version, OS version and architecture, what you expected, the steps that reproduce the issue and the exact error message. Add relevant device or plug-in versions and a diagnostic report if useful. Screenshots should show the relevant state; remove unrelated private information.

For refunds or an inaccessible computer’s activation, include the purchase reference and explain the request. Do not send passwords, payment-card information or a full license key. For integration or redistribution, describe the intended product and distribution.
