01

Scope and local files

The personal-information controller is Echo Vision Lab (에코비전랩), a Korean sole proprietorship, business registration number 111-42-04359. Its address and phone are in the contact section. This policy covers the MixRelay app and Driver, update checks, support and related product-information pages. Lemon Squeezy's own policy also applies to its payment and licensing service.

MixRelay has no account system or usage analytics. Audio, recordings and projects are processed and stored on your computer and are not uploaded to a server. Licensing and update requests do not include recordings, project contents, graphs or audio-device names. Diagnostic reports are saved locally by you. Echo Vision Lab receives a report only if you choose to send it, for example as a support email attachment.

We do not collect your work for advertising or AI training. We do not operate a feature that requests or publishes sensitive personal information, conduct pseudonymized research/statistical processing, or make fully automated decisions with significant effects on you. External plug-ins and services may have their own practices; check their providers' policies.

03

Statutory records

Under Article 6 of the Korean E-Commerce Consumer Protection Act and Article 6 of its Enforcement Decree, the following periods apply to records Echo Vision Lab actually holds and is required to retain. This does not mean we hold every record held by Lemon Squeezy.

  • Contract or withdrawal records: five years. Relevant order identifiers, buyer contact details, product/contract conditions, contract/withdrawal dates and request details.
  • Payment and supply records: five years. Relevant order identifiers, payment amount/date/status, product and supply dates, and refund records.
  • Consumer complaints and dispute-resolution records: three years. Contact details, complaint/inquiry contents and handling results.
  • Advertising/display records: six months. Relevant published product, price, sale conditions and display periods. We do not create user profiles for these records.

Retained records are used only as needed for the retention obligation, record access and dispute handling. If another legal retention duty applies, we identify its law, data and period. Request record access using the rights procedure. See the official guidance on transaction-record retention.

When a retention period ends or information is no longer needed for its purpose, we separate any legally required records and delete the rest without undue delay. Statutory records are stored separately or access-restricted, and deleted when their required period ends.

Electronic files are deleted so they cannot be recovered or reproduced; paper records are shredded or incinerated. Actual deletion cycles for email, attachments, server logs and backups: [Email, log and backup deletion procedures and backup deletion cycle to be confirmed]. We do not remotely delete recordings, projects or diagnostic files on your computer. You control deletion of local files.

Your rights and representatives

You may request access, correction, deletion, restriction of processing and withdrawal of consent-based processing. A legal guardian or lawfully authorized representative may act for you. Where needed, we verify identity and authority using only the minimum information necessary. Do not email a full national ID number, password or full card number.

Email support@mixrelay.com with “Privacy request,” what you want and information needed to locate your records, such as the relevant email/order reference, or use the phone/postal address below. You can also use privacy@dsub.io, the privacy contact in the existing software licenses. No account or diagnostic report is required. We respond within applicable legal deadlines and explain any restriction/refusal and how to challenge it. While handling correction/deletion requests, we do not use or disclose the disputed information except where legally permitted.

Legally required transaction records may not be immediately deletable. Stopping necessary contractual processing can affect the relevant feature, but declining optional files or exercising rights does not itself remove free-use permissions, purchased rights or statutory remedies. For Lemon Squeezy records outside our direct control, contact its privacy channel; we also assist with requests concerning our own records.

Where consent is required for processing a child under 14's information, we follow legal-guardian consent and other applicable procedures. Tell us if unnecessary child data or information processed without required consent has been sent. You must have lawful authority before sending other people's information.
