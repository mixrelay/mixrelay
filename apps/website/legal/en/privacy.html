01

Summary

MixRelay has no accounts, no usage analytics and no ad tracking, and your audio, recordings and projects stay on your computer. Purchase and licence data are processed by Polar (Polar Software, Inc.) as the seller. Echo Vision Lab itself handles only update checks and support email.

02

Purchase and licence data

Checkout information and licence data are processed by Polar Software, Inc. (USA) under the Polar Privacy Policy. The app sends your key and the public organisation and licence-benefit identifiers to api.polar.sh over HTTPS to activate or check your licence. With unlimited computers, the device name stays on your computer and deactivation clears only local credentials. Echo Vision Lab can view order details in the Polar seller dashboard for refunds and support and keeps no separate copy.

03

What Echo Vision Lab processes

  • Update checks: when the app contacts https://appserver.rawsector.com, the server log records the product name, operating system and processor type, and the IP address. They are used to provide updates and handle outages and are deleted automatically after 30 days.
  • Support email: your email address, message and attachments are used to handle your request and kept for 3 years after it is resolved (the dispute-record period under the Korean E-Commerce Act). Diagnostic reports are received only when you send them and are deleted once the problem is solved.

04

Who processes data for us

  • Echo Vision Lab's own servers (Republic of Korea): update server and web pages
  • Cloudflare, Inc. (USA): request delivery and security for the update server and web pages
  • Google LLC (USA, Google Workspace): sending and storing support email

Overseas providers process data only as needed for these services, as disclosed here. Cookies on the web pages follow the rawsector Privacy Policy.

05

Your rights

You can ask to access, correct, delete or stop the processing of your information by writing to privacy@dsub.io; we act without delay. You may also contact the Korean Personal Information Protection Commission or the dispute mediation committee.
