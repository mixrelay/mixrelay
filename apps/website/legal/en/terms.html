04

Supported environments and free features

MixRelay supports macOS 14.2 or later on Apple Silicon and Intel. The latest major macOS version checked with an installed build is currently 27; this does not mean every version and device combination since 14.2 has been tested. Linux requires x86_64 or arm64 and a working user session with PipeWire 1.2 or later and WirePlumber 0.5 or later. Product audio execution in Docker containers or Docker-based VMs is unsupported. See the installation guide for requirements.

The free app has no time limit and does not reduce audio quality or add noise or artificial latency for evaluation. Its limits are:

  • One graph, including saving, loading and restoring it and its mappings.
  • Two physical-input or individual app-capture nodes in total. Virtual-device sources do not count toward this input limit.
  • Two distinct output devices, including virtual outputs. Repeated selections of the same device count once; recorders are excluded.
  • Four channel connections from editing graphs into virtual devices in total; a stereo connection counts as two. Connections between effects, into physical outputs or recorders, and the Driver's basic loopback do not count.
  • Unlimited built-in effects with preset and parameter editing; two external plug-in instances in total, including instances that are off.
  • One recorder, up to two channels and two cumulative hours per recording, excluding paused time.

The free Driver needs no purchase authentication. It provides one default virtual device, up to 64 channels per direction, sample-rate/PCM settings and basic loopback. The app's mapping limit does not restrict channels used by an external DAW with that device.

A paid license removes the free limits on graphs, inputs, outputs, mappings, external plug-ins and recorders, and adds whole-system audio capture and virtual-device creation, removal and renaming. Platform and engine technical limits still apply. Free and paid features use the same product and installer. Returning to free access does not automatically delete excess configurations; free limits apply to new additions and starts. See Free edition and licensing for details.

Uninstalling, reinstalling or deleting local credentials does not itself release a server activation or cancel a purchase. If the old computer is inaccessible or the result is uncertain, ask support for recovery.

Licensing requests send your chosen device name, key and activation ID directly to Lemon Squeezy as needed for the request. See the Privacy Policy for data items and overseas processing.

Korean statutory withdrawal rights

Where Korean e-commerce consumer law applies, ordinary withdrawal is available within seven days of receiving the written contract information, or of supply or the start of supply if later. Statutory extensions apply: for example, seven days from learning, or being able to learn, the seller's address where missing contract information or an address omission prevented withdrawal, or from the end of withdrawal obstruction.

Beginning digital-content supply can limit ordinary withdrawal only where statutory conditions are met, including a clear withdrawal-limitation notice and legally required trial or inspection measures. Unsupplied separable parts remain subject to withdrawal. Trying the free app, accepting terms, receiving a key or downloading does not automatically waive rights. These exceptions are not grounds to refuse our 30-day no-questions refund.

If supply differs from the description, advertising or contract, the statutory withdrawal periods are within three months of supply or within 30 days of learning, or being able to learn, of the discrepancy. Written withdrawal takes effect when sent.

Statutory withdrawal from a digital-content purchase requires reimbursement within three business days of withdrawal, payment-suspension or cancellation measures and applicable statutory delay compensation. No withdrawal penalty or damages for withdrawal are charged. Using Lemon Squeezy does not exclude reimbursement responsibilities imposed on Echo Vision Lab by law.

Send a request to support@mixrelay.com, the order-support channel on your receipt or our business address below. This does not restrict other lawful ways to exercise your rights. Sources: Korean E-Commerce Consumer Protection Act, Article 17 and Article 18.
