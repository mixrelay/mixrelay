01

Purchase and payment

MixRelay paid licences are sold by Polar (Polar Software, Inc.) as merchant of record. Polar handles payment, tax calculation and remittance, receipts and invoices, and payment disputes, and the Polar Buyer Terms govern the purchase. The early-adopter price is US$19 for purchases made before MixRelay 1.0 is released. From the 1.0 release, the price is US$29. Both prices exclude tax; the final amount including tax is shown at checkout before you pay. It is a one-time purchase, not a subscription.

02

Licence and what it includes

Use of the software is governed by the MixRelay licence agreements (app, Driver). A paid licence is for one person, who may activate it on an unlimited number of their own computers. Do not share your key. Purchases made before 1.0 include updates for all 0.x and 1.x versions. Purchases from 1.0 include updates within the purchased major version. Buying a later major version, such as 2.0, is optional. Paid features keep working offline for 30 days after the last successful online check.

03

30-day refund

Ask within 30 days of purchase and we refund you in full, no questions asked.

Send your order number to support@rawsector.com or to the Polar order support address on your receipt. Refunds are processed through Polar and the refunded licence is deactivated. Consumer rights under applicable law apply in addition.

04

Free features

After a refund, device deactivation or licence expiry, the free features (no time limit) and the free Driver keep working, and your recordings, projects and device settings are not deleted.

05

Support

Send product questions to support@rawsector.com. We aim to reply first within 2 business days (Korea).
