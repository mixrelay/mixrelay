# ADR 0002: Private source and commercial option

Status: Accepted

MixRelay begins as freeware distributed from a private source repository. A
future paid offering is planned. Free distribution of binaries does not imply
an open-source grant.

No open-source project license is added until product ownership, third-party
notices, binary distribution terms, and the commercial model are reviewed.
Dependencies remain subject to their respective licenses and notices.
